שלום עולם
The old clock on the mantelpiece ticked away with a slow, steady rhythm that seemed to stretch the seconds into minutes. Outside, the rain beat a relentless tattoo against the windowpane, blurring the distant city lights into smeary streaks of neon yellow and blurry crimson. Sitting by the crackling hearth, Eleanor turned the page of her worn leather journal, the parchment rough against her fingertips. She had spent months tracking down the faint, whispered rumors of the forgotten archive, and now, with the map spread across her knees, the final pieces of the puzzle were finally clicking into place. Every shadow in the room seemed to lean forward in anticipation, as if the house itself were holding its breath to see what secret she would uncover next.
